G-code toolpaths and LightBurn designs for portrait engraving and pen-plotter art — turning photos into machine-ready line work.

This is where the photo-to-machine pipeline lives: converting an image into line art, generating G-code for a pen plotter or CNC, and producing LightBurn designs for laser engraving. The result is machine-ready files rather than hand-drawn paths.

The files are collected on GitHub.
